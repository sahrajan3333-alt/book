Privacy Policy

Privacy by Design: Folio does not transmit your personal reading data, bookmarks, or unpublished drafts to any external server. All state is maintained locally in your browser.

1. Information Stored Locally

To provide a persistent personal reading experience without demanding an account sign-in, Folio utilizes your browser's standard localStorage API to store:

  • Bookmarks: The IDs of your bookmarked classics, summaries, and stories.
  • Published Stories: User stories and chapters you compose in the Writers Community.
  • Chat History: Messages you draft in the local demo Writers Lounge.
  • Reader Preferences: Selected font size, light/sepia/dark theme preference, and audio speech rate.

2. Zero Third-Party Trackers

Folio does not include advertising pixels, behavioral tracking scripts, or analytics cookies. You have complete control to clear your storage at any time via your browser settings or our one-click Bookshelf clear buttons.

3. Future Cloud Integration

When user authentication and cloud synchronization (e.g. Supabase) are introduced in future updates, you will have explicit opt-in choice and comprehensive control over your cloud profile.
